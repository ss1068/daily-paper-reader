## 摘要
量化大语言模型（qLLM）因其低延迟和高能效的优势，正越来越多地部署在边缘设备上。然而，模型量化削弱了对齐安全保障，使得 qLLM 极易受到越狱攻击。为应对这一挑战，我们提出了 MOMAT（多图谱混合框架），这是一种结合了结构化知识检索与低功耗防御加速的硬件增强型安全框架。每个“图谱”（Atlas）代表一组有害或良性样本集及策略模板的语义簇，实现了领域局部的检索增强生成（RAG）防护，从而缓解了大型异构安全数据库中维度灾难及由此产生的语义稀疏性问题。MOMAT 为每个提示词从所有图谱中检索前 k 个相似特征，并利用轻量级专家混合（MoE）检测器进行评估，同时由存内计算（CiM）加速的相似度引擎执行快速、低功耗的图谱局部检索。基于 CiM 的检索将 100 个查询批次的处理时间从 15,052.44 毫秒缩短至 3,207.21 纳秒（加速约 469 万倍），并将能耗从 8.1×10⁷ μJ 降低至 3.32 μJ，较基于 DRAM（树莓派）的基准方案实现了约 25 万倍的能效提升。在标准基准测试上的红队评估表明，MOMAT 在保持与现有最先进方法相当的防御性能的同时，避免了对良性内容的误杀，并提供了显著的效率增益，证明了基于 CiM 的模块化防御能够使边缘部署的 qLLM 既安全又节能。我们将发布包含 223.2k 个样本的完整数据集，以促进后续研究。

## Abstract
Quantized large language models are increasingly deployed on edge devices for their low latency and energy efficiency. However, model quantization weakens alignment safeguards, leaving qLLMs (quantized large language models) highly vulnerable to jailbreak attacks. To address this challenge, we present MOMAT (Mixture of Multiple Atlases), a hardware-enhanced safety framework that combines structured knowledge retrieval with low-power defense acceleration. Each atlas represents a semantic cluster of harmful or benign sample sets and policy templates, enabling domain-localized Retrieval-Augmented Generation guarding that mitigates the curse of dimensionality and the resulting semantic sparsity problem in large, heterogeneous safety databases. MOMAT retrieves top-$k$ similarity features from all atlases for each prompt and evaluates them using a lightweight MoE (Mixture of Experts) detector, while a CiM (Compute-in-Memory)-accelerated similarity engine performs fast, low-power atlas-local retrieval. MOMAT's CiM-based retrieval accelerates a 100-query batch from 15,052.44 ms to 3,207.21 ns (a $4.69 \times 10^6\times$ speedup) and reduces energy from $8.1 \times 10^7$ $μ$J to 3.32 $μ$J, yielding an approximately $2.5 \times 10^5\times$ energy reduction over DRAM-based (Raspberry Pi) baselines. Red-team evaluations across standard benchmarks show that MOMAT matches the defense performance of state-of-the-art methods while avoiding benign overkill and providing substantial efficiency gains, demonstrating that CiM-based modular defenses can make edge-deployed qLLMs both safer and more energy-efficient. We will release the full 223.2k-sample dataset to foster future research.
